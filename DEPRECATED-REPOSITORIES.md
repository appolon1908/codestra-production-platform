# Deprecated repositories

Status: **NONE APPROVED**

No repository is declared deprecated solely because it appears duplicated, inactive, or undocumented. The current evidence does not contain protected owner approval plus runtime non-use, backup, isolated restore, replacement health, and rollback-window completion for any deprecation.

Candidates requiring an explicit lifecycle decision are recorded in `REPOSITORY-LIFECYCLE.md`. They remain `UNSETTLED`, not `DEPRECATED`, and must not be archived, deleted, or excluded from source-authority reconciliation.
