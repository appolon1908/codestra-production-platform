# Critical platform alert

1. Identify the alert name, affected service, environment, and correlation
   reference. Never copy credentials, tokens, customer data, telephone numbers,
   recording URLs, or protected payloads into the incident channel.
2. Acknowledge through the approved alerting mechanism and open the incident
   procedure referenced by the approved escalation policy.
3. Preserve logs and current image/configuration digests before changing state.
4. For DNC, authorization, campaign activation, or configuration uncertainty,
   keep dialing and live writes disabled; unknown DNC state fails closed.
5. Escalate to the secondary owner when the primary response deadline expires.
6. Apply only the smallest documented recovery or rollback action.
7. Confirm the original condition cleared, queued work reconciled, and a
   resolved notification reached the approved recovery receiver.
