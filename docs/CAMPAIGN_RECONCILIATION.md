# Campaign reconciliation

Compare Odoo desired lifecycle/telephony intent with canonical campaign and target rows, then read VICIdial campaign, user group, and inbound group through the restricted adapter. Normalize representations before comparison. Missing or mismatched inactive resources may be proposed for bounded remediation; an unexpected active resource is high risk and must fail closed. New resources are created inactive, without lists, hopper loading, carrier routing, or dialing.

The periodic scheduler targets pending jobs frequently and performs a bounded global scan at a configurable low-impact interval. Events accelerate convergence but are not the sole reconciliation mechanism.
