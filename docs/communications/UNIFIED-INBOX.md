# Unified inbox

Inbox views include all, unassigned, mine, team, waiting on customer, waiting
on internal team, escalated, SLA warning/breached, closed, spam and blocked.
Filters include tenant, workspace, department, channel, agent, AI employee,
priority, status, language, campaign, customer and date range.

Conversations cannot be assigned simultaneously to a human and AI employee.
Routing evaluates tenant/workspace, channel, customer, department, campaign,
language, priority, hours, availability, SLA and consent, producing human, AI,
team, queue, escalation, callback, reject or block outcomes.
