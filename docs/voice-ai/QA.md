# Voice QA and analytics

Structured summaries cover purpose, intent, questions/responses, products,
objections, commitments, appointment/callback, complaint/escalation,
disposition recommendation, follow-up and risk. Sentiment and intent include
confidence and are advisory.

QA requires all greeting, disclosure, identity, script, listening, accuracy,
knowledge, professionalism, empathy, objections, compliance, resolution,
disposition and closing criteria, scored 0-5 with evidence references. AI QA is
advisory until supervisor review. Analytics exclude PII labels.
